Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about timeline, edits, and what to expect during your launch.

How long does setup take?

Most founding members launch within 7–10 days after onboarding content is submitted.

Can I request edits?

Yes. You’ll get one structured revision cycle before launch and can request follow-up updates later.

Do I need technical experience?

No. We designed this for busy clinicians who do not want to manage code or complicated setup steps.

Is this only for private practice?

It works best for solo and small group practices, but we can support larger teams too.
